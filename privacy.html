Privacy Policy for FloatNote Chrome Extension

Last updated: 2026

This Chrome extension does NOT collect, transmit or share any of your personal data or note content.

All notes created in this extension are stored locally in your browser using browser local storage. No data will be sent to external servers.

The password information is only stored locally within your browser and will not be uploaded anywhere.

This extension has no analytics, tracking scripts, and will never collect your browsing history.

If you have any questions, contact via Github repository.
